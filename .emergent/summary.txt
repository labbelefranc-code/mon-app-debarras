**ELEC Category Requirements (Implemented by AI):**
The ELEC category is divided into four main sub-categories:
1.  **ELECTROMENAGER**: Further subdivided into FROID (Frigo, Congélateur, Frigo Combiné), CUISINE (Gazinière, Lave Vaisselle, Four with options, Microondes with options, Petit Électroménager with options), and SALLE DE BAIN (Lave Linge with options, Sèche Linge).
2.  **MULTIMEDIA / ELECTRONIQUE**: Should directly display articles (Télévision/Écran with options, Hifi/Lecteur with quantity, Console de jeux vidéo, Enceintes with options, Ordinateur with options, Imprimante/scanner with options) without an intermediate sub-category page.
3.  **ELECTRIQUE**: Should directly display articles (Aspirateur with options, Éclairage with options, Chauffe-eau/cumulus with a pre-checked 'déjà démonté' option) without an intermediate sub-category page.
4.  **Chauffage/Climatisation/Ventilation**: Further subdivided into CHAUD (Convecteur, Chauffage bain d'huile, Chauffage d'appoint, Poêle à pétrole, Poêle à bois) and FROID (Clim mobile, Clim murale, Partie extérieure clim with options, Ventilateur sur pied, Ventilateur de table, Ventilateur au plafond).

**General Requirements:**
*   Options for articles should appear on the same page (e.g., Four: encastré, taille micro-ondes).
*   Quantity selections (e.g., Hifi/Lecteur) should use a dropdown with numbers (1, 2, 3...).
*   The déjà démonté option for Chauffe-eau / cumulus must be pre-checked and non-deactivatable.
*   The overall goal is a seamless, multi-level hierarchical navigation with accurate article display and options, following patterns established for MOBILIER and JARDIN.
